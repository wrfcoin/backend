# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately to **security@wrfcoin.com**. Please do not open
public issues for vulnerabilities. Public policy: https://wrfcoin.com/security.

The scope is the WRFCoin public testnet. Include the affected component and
revision, reproduction steps, impact, and sanitized evidence. Do not send wallet
recovery phrases or private keys. No bounty or payment is promised.

Test against your own local deployment. Do not disrupt the shared testnet or
access other participants' data. Testnet tokens have no monetary value.
